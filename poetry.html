Collage Poetry

While this class's primary output was media-based documentation of campus histories, an in-class assignment on archival collage poetry was so moving that I wanted to include a separate section to showcase it. These poems draw text and imagery from UCSD Disorientation guides published between 1970 and 2024, some of which you can find here. Disorientation guides, produced by student activist groups as alternatives to the university's official orientation materials, carry their own archive of campus dissent, which formal institutional mechanisms rarely preserve and that largely survives through informal circulation and, more recently, digitization efforts like those by UCSD Radical Archives. Using these guides, students assembled collage poems that layered fragments of decades-old student writing with their own language, treating the guides as historical artifacts they could think and write with. Students were asked to place their poems in and around campus, in sites they felt conversed with their collage poetry.
